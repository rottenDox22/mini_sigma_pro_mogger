FAQ

Frequently asked questions

How fast can we get set up?

Most teams connect their first data source in under 10 minutes. A full production rollout with governance typically takes 1–2 weeks with our onboarding team.

Which data sources do you support?

100+ native connectors including Snowflake, BigQuery, Redshift, Postgres, MySQL, Stripe, Salesforce, HubSpot, and Segment — plus REST APIs and CSV uploads.

Is our data secure?

Yes. Lumen is SOC 2 Type II certified, encrypts data in transit and at rest, and never uses your data to train models. Row-level permissions and SSO are available on all paid plans.

How accurate are the AI answers?

Every answer is grounded in your semantic model and cites the exact queries behind it. Users can flag a bad answer, which feeds directly into improving your model over time.
